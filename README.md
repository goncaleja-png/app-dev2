
# My Favorite Series: Stranger Things

## About the Series
**Stranger Things** is a science fiction and horror series created by the Duffer Brothers. It follows a group of friends who discover supernatural mysteries in their hometown.

## Why I Like It
I like this series because of its interesting storyline, unique characters, and exciting scenes. It combines mystery, adventure, friendship, and horror.
